Question 4 · For every shortlisted vendor

Points to settle before the next round

Send these to Gonghong, Dingming, Tangshi and Taihe with a request for a best-and-final CFR offer within 7 days.

  1. Soft package figures do not reconcile. 160 mm × 1 kg on the 143 mm body is about 0.39 g/cm³, above the cotton band of 0.30–0.35. Decide whether diameter, weight or density governs, and make it the acceptance test.
  2. Hard package figures do not reconcile. 150/180 × 155 mm at 1 kg is about 0.34 g/cm³. At 0.40–0.50 g/cm³ the same cone weighs 1.18–1.48 kg. The sheet already marks this "confirm".
  3. Traverse. Every offer uses 147–152 mm against a 143.00 mm tube body. Ask for a 140 mm drum or setting so yarn stays off the 10.40 and 7.00 mm stepped ends.
  4. Thread winder speed. 1,400 rpm on this bobbin is only 120–350 m/min. Restate the requirement in m/min, for example at least 1,000 m/min.
  5. Voltage. Site supply is 415 V and the RFQ allows 440 V. A 380 V ±10% machine tops out at 418 V. Ask for confirmation at 415 V, or a transformer in the offer.
  6. Corrected soft bobbin drawing. Steps 54.70 / 49.60 / 43.15 at the bottom and 43.15 / 48.80 / 52.90 at the top. Only Charming Star has the corrected version.
  7. Sample tubes and a recorded trial. Ship soft, hard and sewing tubes plus yarn. Require a video before shipment with measured density, weight, dimensions and a full doffing cycle.
  8. Common terms. Signed 21-line checklist, CFR Chittagong price, L/C at sight with about 10% held until commissioning, lead time, warranty from commissioning, installation cost, HS codes.
